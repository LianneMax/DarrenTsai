How FHA works

Four things decide your FHA payment.

These are the numbers underwriting actually runs. Here is what moves each one.

01

Down payment tier

On a $400,000 purchase, 5% down is $20,000 and leaves a $380,000 loan. Below 5% down, annual MIP rises from 0.50% to 0.55%.

02

Upfront MIP is added to the loan

Upfront MIP is 1.75%, or $6,650, financed straight into the loan, so the real balance becomes $386,650.

03

Escrow, HOA, and monthly MIP

Principal and interest is $2,572. Taxes add $410, MIP adds $177, insurance adds $125, and HOA adds $350, pushing the payment to $3,635.

04

Both debt to income ratios

Front end DTI weighs housing cost against income. Back end DTI adds car, student, card, and support payments. Both have to pass.
